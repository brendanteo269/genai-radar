How this works, and where it is weak
  1. Each morning an n8n workflow reads a few announcement feeds and skips anything it has already seen.
  2. Claude fills in the same form for every item: what it does, who it is for, one task to try, a verdict, a catch, and how big the change is.
  3. The card is saved and this page shows it. A bigger change gets a bigger card.

Two labels. “Auto-graded” means Claude read an announcement. Nobody used the tool. “Tested by me” means I tried it and wrote the notes myself.

  • Auto-graded cards can overrate a tool, because announcements are written to impress.
  • The feeds are a small sample, not the whole market.
  • Nothing here is advice on what data is safe to share. Follow your own company’s policy.
